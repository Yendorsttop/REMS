# Repository Governance

- REMS is governed by Founder-reserved authority.
- Preserve constitutional domain boundaries.
- Use a governed modular-monolith architecture.
- Do not represent specifications as executable certification.
- Do not claim operational certification without verified source code, migrations, tests, deployment artifacts, and evidence.
- Never commit credentials or production secrets.
- Record material architectural decisions in ADRs.
- RED-001 exclusively owns executive identity and organizational authority.
